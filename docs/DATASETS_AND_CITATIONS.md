# Dataset releases and citations

Record the exact release, parent capture lineage, publisher citations, and rights before using or sharing a dataset. Acquisition, academic use, commercial use, and redistribution are separate permissions. Keep source data and restricted derivatives in authorized local storage. This catalog draws on the G36/G36.5 citation audit, public release metadata, and the [research archive](research/README.md).

Verify the exact artifact terms and upstream requirements before acquisition or sharing. General redistribution of the UQ derivatives is unverified.
